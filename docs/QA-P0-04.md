## Bugs found and fixed during review

1. **Photographer credit blank (since P0-03).** The image manifest helper dropped the `credit` field, so the Bruges
   caption and the footer credit rendered with an empty name and no link. Fixed; now asserted by QA.
2. **Atmosphere headline at 320px** wrapped as "More than / a / night out". Headline now scales down on the narrowest
   phones.
3. A test assertion read `transform` instead of Tailwind v4's `scale` property; the hover effect itself was fine.

## Known compromises

1. **Still four Zenzo photographs.** The new gallery uses more varied crops (narrow tall, panorama, details), but the
   same moments still recur — the bucket-and-sparkler frame is in the gallery twice and VIP 01.
2. **Colour grade** remains a design treatment applied to Zenzo's photographs (reversible with `grade: 'none'`).
3. **Location photo is still a stock image.** It is now darker, warmer and cinematic, and sits behind the address, but
   it is not Zenzo's. Captioned and credited.
4. **VIP hover on short panels:** when the inclusions open on the desktop covers, the title moves up close beneath the
   masthead number. Fine at 1440 and 1024; it is the tightest point of the layout.
5. **Hero softness** above ~1700px wide (source photographs are ~1600px).
6. **Not verified:** Safari/Firefox, a physical phone, a screen reader; axe covers only part of accessibility.
7. Logo is the official file, unaltered, so its pale yellow-gold sits slightly apart from the champagne UI gold.

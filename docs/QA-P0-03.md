## Known visual compromises

1. **Only four Zenzo photographs exist on the site.** Every image is one of them, cropped differently, so the same
   moments recur: the bucket-and-sparkler frame appears in the gallery twice and in VIP 01; the confetti crowd is the
   hero, a gallery tile and VIP 03. A second photoshoot would fix this more than any design change.
2. **Colour grade.** The originals are violet, green and magenta; they are remapped to ember / noir so the page reads
   black-red-amber-gold like the reference. This is a design treatment, documented and reversible (`grade: 'none'`),
   but it is not the photographs as Zenzo published them.
3. **Reference imagery that doesn't exist for Zenzo:** the sparkler-bottle hero crowd, the portrait of a woman in
   sunglasses, the Z-sign building exterior and the video thumbnail. The closest Zenzo frames stand in; the exterior
   is a Bruges stock photograph (the one non-Zenzo image, credited).
4. **Source resolution.** The photographs are ~1600px wide, so the hero is soft on screens wider than ~1700px.
5. **Logo colour.** Zenzo's real logo is a paler yellow-gold than the champagne used in the UI. It is used unaltered, as
   asked, so the two golds sit slightly apart. Its fine lines also read small at 52px tall in the header.
6. **Language selector** is present but NL/FR are disabled — no translations exist yet.
7. **Social links** are text links to Zenzo's own profiles (as linked from zenzobar.be), not the reference's icons.
8. **Not verified:** Safari/Firefox, a physical phone, a screen reader; axe covers only part of accessibility.
